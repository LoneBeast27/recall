## Recall 0.1.11 for Windows

**Improved**
- **Clips open faster**: a clip that Recall keeps a copy of on your SSD now opens without the setup steps it used to wait on.
- **Lighter on low-end PCs**: Recall holds its background work while you are not using it, so it takes less from your games.
- **Library grouping and grid size**: you can group clips by time, day, month, year or game, and small groups share a row. Grouping is off by default. You can also choose how many tiles sit across the grid: Auto, or 3 to 8.
- **More dependable updates**: Recall can now fetch updates from more than one place, and it checks the signature of every update before it installs it.

**Fixed**
- **Function keys in shortcuts**: shortcuts can now use the function keys. A combination that Windows keeps for itself, such as Alt+F4, is refused, and Recall shows one line that explains why and offers a fix.
- **Clearer update messages**: when Recall cannot reach its update page, it now says it couldn't check for updates, instead of saying you are up to date.

**Install**
Download `RecallSetup.exe` and run it. An installed Recall offers the update by itself; your settings are kept, and settings from every earlier version load unchanged. During the update, a recording you started is stopped and saved first; the replay buffer is not saved.

**Unsigned installer**
This release is not code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC": choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**. See the [code signing policy](https://github.com/LoneBeast27/recall/blob/main/CODE_SIGNING_POLICY.md) for how to check the download.

`SHA256SUMS.txt` lists the installer's checksum, and `SOURCE-REVISION.txt` names the exact source commit.
