## Recall 0.1.10 for Windows

**New**
- **Full screen for clips**: a button at the bottom right of the player (or F) fills the screen with the video and its playback controls. F11 makes the whole Recall window full screen on any page. Esc, F or the button leave the video's full screen; F11 again, or the exit button in the title bar, leaves the window's. The window comes back where it was.

**Improved**
- **Scroll bars you can grab**: the clips list, the Settings pages and the editor's export panel show a scroll bar you can drag, or click to jump.
- **Seeking in a clip that is still loading is quicker**: for a clip still loading from a hard drive, Recall fetches the part you jumped to first instead of finishing what it was loading.
- **Plain update messages**: looking for, downloading and installing an update now reads as simple steps, and when something goes wrong Recall says what happened in one plain sentence.

**Fixed**
- **Recording size follows the game**: a game that starts in a small window and then goes fullscreen or borderless is now recorded at full size from that moment, instead of staying at the small window's size. After that the clip size stays put: later resolution changes are fitted into it. A replay saved in the first moments after that switch holds only what came after it.
- **Recall's pill over games in exclusive fullscreen**: when Recall records a game through desktop capture and the game switches to exclusive fullscreen, Recall now moves to window capture after about 2 seconds, which lets the pill show over the game. It remembers the game, so next time it starts that way, and moves back after the game has been borderless for 10 seconds. The replay buffer is kept across the switch; the switch leaves a gap of under a second. On PCs where Windows does not allow Recall to hide it, a yellow capture border shows around the game on screen while window capture is used (it is not in the recording).

**Install**
Download `RecallSetup.exe` and run it. An installed Recall offers the update by itself; your settings are kept, and settings from every earlier version load unchanged. During the update, a recording you started is stopped and saved first; the replay buffer is not saved.

**Unsigned installer**
This release is not code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC": choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**. See the [code signing policy](https://github.com/LoneBeast27/recall/blob/main/CODE_SIGNING_POLICY.md) for how to check the download.

`SHA256SUMS.txt` lists the installer's checksum, and `SOURCE-REVISION.txt` names the exact source commit.
