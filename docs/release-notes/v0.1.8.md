## Recall 0.1.8 for Windows

**Fixed**
- **Exports keep every audio track**: a track recorded below 48 kHz (some mics and headsets) played back chopped in a mixed export, a track without channel labels made every export fail, and a lossless export of a clip with PCM or Vorbis audio failed. All three now export. A track above 96 kHz kept as its own track also exports now, at 96 kHz (88.2 kHz for 176.4 kHz audio).
- **Lossless export keeps that audio lossless**: audio that MP4 can't hold as it is (PCM, Vorbis, TrueHD, DTS) is stored as FLAC; the video is still copied untouched. 16 and 24-bit audio is bit for bit identical; 32-bit and float audio is stored at 24 bits.

**Install**
Download `RecallSetup.exe` and run it. An installed Recall offers the update by itself; your settings are kept, and settings from every earlier version load unchanged. During the update, a recording in progress is stopped and saved.

**Unsigned installer**
This release is not code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC": choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**. See the [code signing policy](https://github.com/LoneBeast27/recall/blob/main/CODE_SIGNING_POLICY.md) for how to check the download.

`SHA256SUMS.txt` lists the installer's checksum, and `SOURCE-REVISION.txt` names the exact source commit.
