## Recall 0.1.5 for Windows

**What's new**
- **Upscaled export**: a clip recorded below its game's resolution can be exported larger, up to the game's resolution, from **Advanced** on the export page. Recall uses NVIDIA RTX Video Super Resolution when it is turned on in the NVIDIA app (System › Video › RTX Video Enhancement › Super resolution), else AMD FidelityFX Super Resolution 1 (also for clips under 640×360), else a plain resize. Exports wider or taller than 4096 pixels are saved as HEVC, and the export page says so.
- **Report a problem** (Settings › General): describe what went wrong and Recall saves a zip on your PC, and can open a new GitHub issue in your browser with your description; you attach the zip yourself. Recall uploads nothing. The zip can hold an action trace and crash records in fixed words and numbers, a settings summary of numbers and switches, and up to 6 screenshots or clips you add with **+ Add media**, with their file metadata removed. It never holds paths, window titles, or clip, game, program, device or user names, and a confirm step lists every file first.
- **Recording resolution** (Settings › Recording): **Native** records each game at its own size and shape; a lower choice keeps the shape, so 16:10 and ultrawide games are never squeezed or padded.

**Improved**
- **Exports use the graphics card** for decoding and resizing too, so they use far less processor time; downscaled exports are sharper.
- **Export page**: everyday options come first, set-once options are under **Advanced**. With a size limit, **Smooth motion** keeps 720p or more and lowers the frame rate first.
- **Minecraft: Java Edition** is recognised by its game window, and all its clips share one "Minecraft" folder.

**Fixed**
- Windowed games are recorded at their own size, with no black bars.
- Recordings wider than the encoder takes are fitted to its limit instead of failing.
- A clip's frame rate is read as recorded, so dips during play no longer make it read as about 50 fps or cap its export.
- The space bar types a space again in clip titles, search and other text boxes.

**Install**
Download `RecallSetup.exe` and run it. An installed Recall offers the update by itself; your settings are kept, and settings from every earlier version load unchanged. During the update, a recording in progress is stopped and saved.

**Unsigned installer**
This release is not code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC": choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**. See the [code signing policy](https://github.com/LoneBeast27/recall/blob/main/CODE_SIGNING_POLICY.md) for how to check the download.

`SHA256SUMS.txt` lists the installer's checksum, and `SOURCE-REVISION.txt` names the exact source commit.
